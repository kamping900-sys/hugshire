# Research and evidence

This document lists the external research behind Hugshire's problem statement, how each finding shaped the product, and how external evidence differs from Hugshire's own metrics.

**Rules followed:**
- Every figure is quoted with its source, date, geography and population.
- Figures from different populations are never combined.
- No Hugshire outcome is claimed from external research.

---

## 1. YouGov: why Americans donate to charity, and why they don't

- **Source:** YouGov, published **October 26, 2026**. https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/55814-why-americans-donate-to-charity-and-why-they-dont
- **Method:** online survey (YouGov Surveys: Serviced), fieldwork **August 20–21, 2026**. The sample is **1,291 U.S. adults** aged 18+, weighted by age, race, gender, education and region to all U.S. adults.

| Finding | Population |
|---|---|
| **41%** say they cannot afford to donate (the leading reason) | U.S. adults who **had not donated** money to charity in the past 12 months |
| **48%** say feeling pressured or guilted into giving would discourage them | U.S. adults |
| **43%** say not knowing how their money will be used would discourage them | U.S. adults |
| **40%** say receiving too many requests or follow-up messages would discourage them | U.S. adults |
| **39%** say clear evidence of how donations are used would encourage future giving | U.S. adults |
| **52%** say not trusting a charity would make them less likely to donate | U.S. adults |

**Notes:**
- The 41% applies **only** to non-donors. It does not mean 41% of all people cannot afford to give.
- The 39% for "clear evidence encourages giving" is a different item from the page's separate 39% about unwanted street, phone or door-to-door fundraising.

## 2. Fundraising Effectiveness Project (FEP): more dollars, fewer donors

- **Source:** Association of Fundraising Professionals (AFP), Fundraising Effectiveness Project release. https://afpglobal.org/news/fundraising-effectiveness-project-reports-strongest-revenue-growth-five-years-even-fewer
- **Corroborating coverage** of the full-year 2025 report:
  - [NonProfit PRO](https://www.nonprofitpro.com/article/fundraising-growth-in-2025-continues-to-mask-a-shrinking-donor-base/)
  - [The Chronicle of Philanthropy](https://www.philanthropy.com/solutions/giving-usa-reports-a-record-high-but-heres-what-it-misses/)

**Findings (2025 vs 2024, U.S. nonprofits in the FEP dataset):**

| Measure | Change |
|---|---|
| Estimated dollars raised | **+5.0%** (the strongest growth in five years) |
| Estimated number of donors | **−3.6%** |

Coverage reports the steepest donor losses among micro and small donors. Overall charitable revenue **grew**; what fell was the number of people giving. This does not mean total giving declined.

## 3. Charities Aid Foundation (CAF): World Giving Report 2026

- **Source:** CAF press release, **June 3, 2026**. https://www.cafonline.org/home/about-us/press-office/world-giving-report-reveals-factors-that-increase-generosity-to-good-causes
- **Coverage:** more than 60,000 people in 105 countries.

| Finding | Scope |
|---|---|
| **61%** of people donated in the past year (directly to people in need, to charity, or for religious reasons), down from 64% in 2024 | global |
| On average people gave about **1%** of their income | global |
| Countries where **over 80%** feel a strong sense of belonging to their local community give **nearly three times more** than countries where belonging is low | country comparison |
| **56%** support charities working locally and **55%** nationally, versus **22%** working across many countries | global |

## 4. From evidence to product design

| Evidence | Barrier | Hugshire design response |
|---|---|---|
| 41% of non-donors can't afford to give (YouGov) | Giving means losing money you may need | **Yield-powered giving:** principal stays attributable to the supporter and can be requested back; only yield supports causes |
| 48% are put off by pressure, 40% by too many requests (YouGov) | Appeal fatigue | **Continuous, opt-in support:** daily auto-send of ripe yield; no repeated asks; ads are optional and earn nothing for watching |
| 43% are discouraged by not knowing how money is used; 39% encouraged by clear evidence (YouGov) | Opacity | **Verifiable transfers** (onchain records, confirmed payouts) next to **Notice Board** updates, labelled "Reported" or "Verified" |
| More dollars but fewer donors (FEP) | Support concentrated in fewer donors | **Broader participation:** a shared game world, and a **Community Heart Pool** that gives every eligible supporter an equal share to direct |
| Belonging is linked to much higher generosity (CAF) | Giving feels isolated | **Cooperative towns:** each cause has a shared town that supporters build together, creating a sense of community around it |
| Most support goes to local and national causes (CAF) | Distance from causes | **A town per organisation**, with its own story and updates |

## 5. External research vs Hugshire metrics

| | External research | Hugshire metrics |
|---|---|---|
| What it shows | Why people give or don't, in general populations | What happens on Hugshire (transfers, participation, organisation reports) |
| Used for | The problem statement and design choices | Impact reporting after launch |
| Claims | None about Hugshire's effect | Only once measured: no retention or giving-uplift claims before data exists |

The impact measurement framework is in [Impact and sustainability](IMPACT_AND_SUSTAINABILITY.md#4-measurement-framework-proposed).
